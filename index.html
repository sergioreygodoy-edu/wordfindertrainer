Exam Strategy Tip
  • The grammatical category must match the clue. “to + infinitive” needs an infinitive verb; a plural clue needs a plural noun; an -ing clue needs an -ing form; an adjective clue needs an adjective.
  • Copy verbatim. Write the word or expression exactly as it appears in the text. Do not change tense, plural or spelling.
  • Write only what is asked: one word or two, with no extra words. Click a word in the text to insert it without typos.
